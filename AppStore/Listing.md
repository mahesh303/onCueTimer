## Description (4000)
SpeechCue Timer puts a clear countdown and your cues in front of the speaker. Connect your iPad to a stage monitor, TV or projector with a cable or AirPlay, and the speaker sees the time left while you run the show from the iPad.

TIME, AT A GLANCE
• A large countdown made to be read from the stage
• Turns yellow at your wrap-up point and red when time is up
• Count into overtime (−00:35) to show how far over a talk has run, or stop at 00:00
• Add or remove 10 seconds while the timer runs
• Save up to four quick times for the talk lengths you use most

CUE THE SPEAKER
• Send a message to the stage screen in one tap
• Zap shakes the message to catch the speaker's eye, and the Zap button glows when time runs out
• Type a message during the talk and it goes up on its own when the timer reaches zero
• Keep four saved messages ready, starting with "Times up!"
• Full emoji keyboard

RUN A SHOW FROM SEVERAL IPADS
• Link iPads with a 4-digit show code; no internet connection or account needed
• Every linked iPad shows the same countdown and message, and any of them can run the show
• Quick times, saved messages, text size and warning settings stay in step across iPads
• An iPad that joins late picks up the show that's already running

MAKE IT YOURS
• Two layouts: Glass Console, with a large preview and floating controls, and Stage Ring, with a big progress ring
• Choose the background colour behind the timer
• Scale the speaker's text from 50% to 200%
• Built-in user guide

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. SpeechCue Timer collects no data. Settings stay on your iPad, and sync talks only to your own iPads nearby.

Requires an iPad with iPadOS 18.2 or later. A second screen (connected by HDMI or AirPlay) is needed to show the speaker display.

## Review notes (App Review Information → Notes)
SpeechCue Timer is a countdown timer for live events. The operator runs it on an iPad and the speaker sees the countdown and messages on an external display (HDMI or AirPlay). Without an external display, the operator screen shows a live preview of what the speaker would see.

Sync (the circular-arrows button at the top of the screen) links iPads on the same 4-digit show code using MultipeerConnectivity. It needs a second iPad to test; everything else works on one iPad. No account or sign-in is required.
