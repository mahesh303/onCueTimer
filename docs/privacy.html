Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

SpeechCue Timer does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your iPad

Your settings (such as the skin, background colour, text size, warning time, show code, the name you give your iPad, your recently used emoji, and a random ID that lets your own iPads recognise each other when syncing) are saved on your iPad only. Timer times and messages are kept in memory while the app is open. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Sync between iPads

If you turn on Sync, the app uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity to connect directly to other iPads nearby that use the same show code, over Wi-Fi, peer-to-peer Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. The connection is encrypted. It carries only the show: the timer, the message on screen, quick times, saved messages, text size and warning settings. Nothing passes through our servers or the internet. iPadOS asks for Local Network permission the first time; sync works only if you allow it, and you can turn it off at any time.

No tracking, analytics or ads

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or third-party code that collects data, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
